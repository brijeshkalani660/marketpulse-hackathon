# 2-minute demo script (screen recording with your voice, no editing needed)

**0:00 to 0:15, the problem.** "Many sites show one confident number for where a stock is going. For a beginner that looks like a fact. I wanted to know if a simple model can be trusted, and I wanted to show how wrong it can be."

**0:15 to 1:15, the demo (about 60 seconds).** Open MarketPulse, open the Apple page. Point at: the price chart, the blue estimate line, the shaded range, and the "Range confidence" badge. Say: "A past price goes in, and an estimate with a likely range comes out: about plus 2% in 3 months, but anywhere from minus 14% to plus 21%." Hover an amber news dot and a grey ring on the chart.

**1:15 to 1:45, how it works and the test.** Open the notebook with outputs. "I tested it on later dates the model never saw: 4,823 cases. The range held 78.9% of the time against a 80% target. But the middle estimate was no better than guessing no change, and a machine-learning model did not beat simple baselines."

**1:45 to 2:00, next steps.** "It fails for the most volatile stocks, at 72.6%, and my data only includes stocks that still exist. Next I want to test whether news headlines add anything. This is a prototype, not financial advice."
